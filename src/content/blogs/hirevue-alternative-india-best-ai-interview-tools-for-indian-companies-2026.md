Hiring in India is very different from hiring anywhere else.

The scale is larger.
The application volume is heavier.
And the pressure to hire fast is constant.

A single job opening can easily attract hundreds — sometimes thousands — of applications within days. For recruiters, this creates a serious operational challenge. Screening resumes manually, scheduling introductory calls, and conducting repetitive first-round interviews quickly becomes difficult to manage.

This is exactly why AI interview platforms are becoming one of the fastest-growing categories in recruitment technology.

Over the last few years, tools like HireVue gained global attention for automating video interviews and streamlining candidate evaluation. But as Indian hiring workflows continue evolving, many recruiters and startups are now actively searching for a HireVue alternative in India that better fits local recruitment needs, campus hiring scale, and startup hiring speed.

The shift is not just about replacing interviews with AI. It is about making hiring more structured, scalable, and efficient.

One of the biggest reasons Indian companies look for AI video interview platforms is the growing workload on recruiters. Traditional hiring processes still depend heavily on manual screening and live introductory interviews. Recruiters spend hours repeating the same conversations, reviewing resumes, coordinating schedules, and trying to shortlist candidates quickly.

And despite all that effort, strong candidates still get missed.

Modern AI interview software changes this process completely.

Instead of scheduling first-round calls manually, companies can automate the entire first stage of hiring. Candidates complete structured async interviews on their own schedule, while recruiters review organized responses, scores, and evaluation insights in one place.

This creates a hiring workflow that is significantly faster and easier to scale.

For Indian companies, this becomes especially useful during:

* Campus recruitment drives
* High-volume hiring
* Startup expansion hiring
* Remote recruitment
* Technical hiring workflows
