Trust is the foundation of any hire. Yet, recent data exposes a harsh reality: **1 in 8 resumes contain fake or highly exaggerated claims.** While recruiters spend hours trying to find the perfect match, they are often reviewing works of fiction.

## The Anatomy of a Fake Resume

Candidates know what the ATS (Applicant Tracking System) and recruiters want to see. To get past the initial gatekeepers, many resort to:

- **Fake Degrees:** Claiming credentials from prestigious universities they never attended.
- **Exaggerated Experience:** Turning a 3-month internship into a 'Senior Lead' role.
- **Made-Up Projects:** Stealing credit for team achievements or entirely fabricating portfolio work.

## The Cost of Polished Profiles

The biggest tragedy of fake resumes isn't just that you might hire a liar. It's the **Opportunity Cost**. When a recruiter is dazzled by a perfectly keyword-stuffed, exaggerated resume, a genuine, highly capable candidate gets pushed to the bottom of the pile. Polished profiles get shortlisted; genuine candidates get overlooked.
