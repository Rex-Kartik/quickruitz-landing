Top talent doesn't wait around. In today's competitive job market, the half-life of a great candidate is incredibly short. If your hiring process is bogged down by delays, you aren't just frustrating candidates—you are actively losing them to your competitors.

## The 10-Day Death Zone

Our research shows that a delay of just **10 days** between interview and decision is enough to lose your best applicants. Where does this time go?

- **Decision Delay:** Hiring managers taking days to review interview feedback.
- **Waiting for Approvals:** Bureaucratic red tape holding up the official offer letter.
- **Scheduling Nightmares:** Trying to find a 30-minute slot where 4 different directors are free.

While your HR team is navigating internal calendars, your candidate is actively exploring other offers.

## The Consequence of 'Being Too Slow to Win'

When decision delays happen, the outcome is predictable:

1. **Top Candidate Drops Off:** They assume you aren't interested or they get tired of the lack of communication.
2. **Competitor Offer Accepted:** The agile startup down the street makes a faster decision, and you lose out.
