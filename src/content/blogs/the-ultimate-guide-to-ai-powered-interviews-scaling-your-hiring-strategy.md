## 1. What is an AI Interviewing Platform?

An AI interviewing platform leverages artificial intelligence to automate candidate screening, conduct interviews, and evaluate responses efficiently.

Instead of repetitive manual screening calls, candidates participate in asynchronous video interviews. The AI then analyzes responses based on predefined job criteria and provides recruiters with a ranked shortlist of top candidates.

This approach focuses on **skills, performance, and data-driven insights**, rather than just resume keywords.

## 2. Core Benefits of Automated Recruitment

### ⏱ Massive Time Savings  
The average hiring process takes weeks. AI tools can screen hundreds of candidates in minutes, significantly reducing time-to-hire.

### 🌍 24/7 Candidate Experience  
Candidates can complete interviews anytime, improving engagement and reducing drop-offs.

### 📈 Unlimited Scalability  
Whether hiring for 1 role or 100, AI platforms handle large volumes without increasing HR workload.

## 3. Standardization & Bias Reduction

One of the biggest advantages of an AI interviewing platform like QuickRuit is consistency.

Human interviews vary due to mood, bias, or inconsistent questioning. AI ensures:
- Same questions for every candidate  
- Same evaluation criteria  
- Fair comparison across applicants  

By focusing purely on candidate responses and skills, AI helps eliminate unconscious bias and promotes merit-based hiring.
